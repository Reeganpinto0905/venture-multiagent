# Pramp

**Category:** Competitor Intelligence Profile  
**Source Document:** `competitor_intelligence_detailed.pdf` (Pages 1, 2)

## Industry Context
Free peer-to-peer mock interview platform, later acquired by Exponent, matching two candidates to interview each other in turn.

## Product Offering
Scheduled peer-matching sessions across roles (software engineering, data science, product management) with structured interview guides.

## Competitive Strengths
Zero cost to users made it a strong top-of-funnel acquisition channel; peer-to-peer model scales without hiring interviewers.

## Weaknesses & Vulnerabilities
Interview quality depends on the peer partner's skill, which is inconsistent; monetization was weak as a standalone product, which contributed to its acquisition.

## Competitive Analysis & Moat
Was the free-tier counterpart to Interviewing.io's paid, expert-led model; folded into Exponent's paid course ecosystem post-acquisition.

## Due Diligence Benchmarking Use
Illustrates a common edtech pattern: a free community-driven tool acquired for its user funnel rather than standalone profitability.
