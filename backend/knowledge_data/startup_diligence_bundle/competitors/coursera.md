# Coursera

**Category:** Competitor Intelligence Profile  
**Source Document:** `competitor_intelligence_detailed.pdf` (Pages 2)

## Industry Context
Large-scale MOOC platform partnering with universities and companies to offer courses, professional certificates, and online degrees.

## Product Offering
Course catalog spanning nearly every academic and professional subject, verified certificates, and full online degree programs with accredited universities.

## Competitive Strengths
Strong university partnerships lend credibility; broad subject coverage beyond tech; enterprise offering (Coursera for Business) adds a second revenue stream.

## Weaknesses & Vulnerabilities
Not focused on interview prep specifically, so it competes only indirectly with the niche platforms above; completion rates for free MOOCs are historically low.

## Competitive Analysis & Moat
Competes with Udemy and UpGrad on breadth, and with edX historically; differentiates through accredited-degree partnerships rather than low-cost individual courses.

## Due Diligence Benchmarking Use
Relevant as an adjacent-market comparable when a startup's edtech thesis depends on university credibility rather than practice-based skill-building.
