# Exponent

**Category:** Competitor Intelligence Profile  
**Source Document:** `competitor_intelligence_detailed.pdf` (Pages 2)

## Industry Context
Subscription video-course and community platform for interview prep across product management, data science, and engineering roles.

## Product Offering
Structured video courses, a searchable question bank with sample answers, and a community forum, later combined with Pramp's peer mock-interview feature.

## Competitive Strengths
Broader role coverage than pure-engineering competitors (notably strong in PM interview prep); subscription model gives recurring revenue; owns both content and practice via the Pramp acquisition.

## Weaknesses & Vulnerabilities
Course content requires continuous updates to stay current with interview trends; competes against free YouTube/blog content for the same audience.

## Competitive Analysis & Moat
Positioned above LeetCode/HackerRank (which are engineering-only and largely free) and below Interviewing.io (fully human-led); the acquisition of Pramp consolidated its practice offering.

## Due Diligence Benchmarking Use
A useful comparable for evaluating subscription-based interview-prep startups on content depth versus community-driven practice tools.
