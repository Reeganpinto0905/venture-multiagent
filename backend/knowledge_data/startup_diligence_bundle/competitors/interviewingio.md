# Interviewing.io

**Category:** Competitor Intelligence Profile  
**Source Document:** `competitor_intelligence_detailed.pdf` (Pages 1)

## Industry Context
Live, human mock-interview platform pairing candidates with real engineers from companies like Google and Meta.

## Product Offering
Anonymized video/audio mock interviews with actual industry engineers, plus interview recordings candidates can review afterward.

## Competitive Strengths
High realism since interviews are conducted by real hiring engineers; anonymization reduces bias; strong reputation among senior engineering candidates.

## Weaknesses & Vulnerabilities
Higher price point than self-serve practice tools; limited scalability since it depends on recruiting and scheduling real interviewers; smaller total user base than content-heavy competitors.

## Competitive Analysis & Moat
Occupies a premium, high-touch niche distinct from LeetCode/HackerRank's self-serve model; closest comparable is Pramp before its acquisition by Exponent.

## Due Diligence Benchmarking Use
A template for due diligence on marketplace-style interview platforms: unit economics hinge on interviewer supply and retention, not just content volume.
