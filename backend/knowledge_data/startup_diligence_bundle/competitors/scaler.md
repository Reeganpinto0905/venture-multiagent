# Scaler

**Category:** Competitor Intelligence Profile  
**Source Document:** `competitor_intelligence_detailed.pdf` (Pages 4)

## Industry Context
India-focused, cohort-based upskilling program (from InterviewBit's parent company) targeting working software engineers who want career growth.

## Product Offering
Long-form, high-touch cohort courses (often 6+ months) combining live classes, mentorship, and placement assistance, priced at a premium versus self-serve platforms.

## Competitive Strengths
High-touch, outcome-oriented model with real mentors and placement support commands premium pricing and strong completion rates versus passive video courses.

## Weaknesses & Vulnerabilities
Small addressable cohort size per batch limits scale versus self-serve competitors; premium pricing narrows the addressable market to serious career switchers.

## Competitive Analysis & Moat
Competes with Great Learning and UpGrad on cohort-based upskilling, but targets working engineers specifically rather than a broader professional audience.

## Due Diligence Benchmarking Use
A good comparable for evaluating high-touch, high-price, high-retention edtech models versus low-price, high-volume self-serve platforms.
