# UpGrad

**Category:** Competitor Intelligence Profile  
**Source Document:** `competitor_intelligence_detailed.pdf` (Pages 7)

## Industry Context
India-based higher-education platform partnering with universities to deliver online degrees and professional certificate programs.

## Product Offering
Full online degree programs and long-form certificate programs co-created with partner universities, spanning management, technology, and data fields.

## Competitive Strengths
Deep university partnerships (domestic and international) enable degree-granting programs that pure content platforms cannot offer.

## Weaknesses & Vulnerabilities
Long program durations and higher price points create longer payback periods on customer acquisition spend versus short-course competitors.

## Competitive Analysis & Moat
Competes most directly with Great Learning on university-partnered upskilling, and indirectly with Coursera's degree offerings.

## Due Diligence Benchmarking Use
A relevant comparable when the due-diligence question is how much a startup's moat depends on exclusive university partnerships.
