# Byju's

**Category:** Competitor Intelligence Profile  
**Source Document:** `competitor_intelligence_detailed.pdf` (Pages 3)

## Industry Context
India-based K-12 and test-prep edtech company delivering video-led lessons through a mobile app, once India's most highly valued startup.

## Product Offering
App-based video lessons, adaptive practice, and live-class add-ons for school curricula and competitive-exam prep, sold heavily through in-person sales agents.

## Competitive Strengths
Extremely strong brand recognition in India built through aggressive marketing and acquisitions (including Aakash, WhiteHat Jr, and Osmo).

## Weaknesses & Vulnerabilities
Aggressive, sometimes controversial door-to-door and loan-based sales tactics drew regulatory scrutiny; rapid acquisition-led growth left the company overleveraged, leading to a severe valuation collapse, layoffs, and insolvency proceedings.

## Competitive Analysis & Moat
Once the category leader against Unacademy and Vedantu in India's live/video learning space; its financial distress reshuffled the competitive landscape significantly.

## Due Diligence Benchmarking Use
A central due-diligence case study on overexpansion: rapid acquisitions and aggressive sales financing can mask weak underlying unit economics until a funding environment tightens.
