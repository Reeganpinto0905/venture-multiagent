# AlgoExpert

**Category:** Competitor Intelligence Profile  
**Source Document:** `competitor_intelligence_detailed.pdf` (Pages 5)

## Industry Context
Subscription video platform built by a single ex-FAANG engineer, offering structured walkthroughs of curated coding-interview questions.

## Product Offering
A curated (not exhaustive) set of coding questions each with a detailed video explanation, plus adjacent SystemsExpert and MLExpert product lines.

## Competitive Strengths
High production quality and a curated, non-overwhelming question set appeals to candidates who find LeetCode's sheer volume intimidating.

## Weaknesses & Vulnerabilities
Much smaller question bank than LeetCode limits long-term engagement; built and marketed around a single personal brand, creating founder-dependency risk.

## Competitive Analysis & Moat
Positions itself as a premium, curated alternative to LeetCode's volume-first approach; expanded into adjacent exam categories to diversify beyond pure DSA prep.

## Due Diligence Benchmarking Use
A good example of founder-brand-driven, curation-first positioning as a competitive strategy against a much larger incumbent.
