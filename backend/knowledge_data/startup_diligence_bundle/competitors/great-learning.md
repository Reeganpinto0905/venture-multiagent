# Great Learning

**Category:** Competitor Intelligence Profile  
**Source Document:** `competitor_intelligence_detailed.pdf` (Pages 6, 7)

## Industry Context
Professional upskilling platform offering cohort-based programs in data science, AI, and management, often via university partnerships.

## Product Offering
Blended online/live cohort programs, many co-branded with universities, targeting working professionals seeking career transitions into tech and analytics.

## Competitive Strengths
University co-branding lends credibility; cohort structure with mentorship supports stronger completion and outcome rates than passive self-paced courses.

## Weaknesses & Vulnerabilities
Premium, longer-duration programs mean a longer sales cycle and higher price point, narrowing the addressable audience versus low-cost marketplaces.

## Competitive Analysis & Moat
Directly comparable to UpGrad and Simplilearn in the cohort-based professional-upskilling category; differentiates through specific university partnership mix.

## Due Diligence Benchmarking Use
Useful comparable for assessing university-partnership-dependent upskilling models and their reliance on partner-brand credibility.
