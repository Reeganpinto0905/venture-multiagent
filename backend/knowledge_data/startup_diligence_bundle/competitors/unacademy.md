# Unacademy

**Category:** Competitor Intelligence Profile  
**Source Document:** `competitor_intelligence_detailed.pdf` (Pages 3, 4)

## Industry Context
India-based live-class platform for competitive exam preparation (civil services, engineering entrance, and other national exams).

## Product Offering
Live online classes taught by an educator marketplace, subscription plans, and recorded content libraries organized by exam category.

## Competitive Strengths
Educator-marketplace model lets it scale content across many exam categories quickly; strong brand among competitive-exam aspirants in India.

## Weaknesses & Vulnerabilities
High marketing and educator-payout costs relative to subscription revenue; faced multiple rounds of layoffs as it worked toward profitability.

## Competitive Analysis & Moat
Direct competitor to Byju's in India's live-learning segment, but exam-prep-focused rather than K-12-focused; also competes with smaller regional exam-prep apps.

## Due Diligence Benchmarking Use
Useful for assessing marketplace-model edtech, where educator supply and payout economics matter as much as student demand.
