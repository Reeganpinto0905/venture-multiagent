# Udemy

**Category:** Competitor Intelligence Profile  
**Source Document:** `competitor_intelligence_detailed.pdf` (Pages 2, 3)

## Industry Context
Open marketplace where independent instructors publish and sell video courses on virtually any topic, including coding and interview prep.

## Product Offering
Pay-per-course marketplace model (rather than subscription) with frequent deep discounting, plus a separate Udemy Business subscription for enterprises.

## Competitive Strengths
Enormous course catalog and low individual price points; instructors can launch a course with minimal barriers, creating fast content growth.

## Weaknesses & Vulnerabilities
Inconsistent course quality since there is little curation; heavy reliance on discount pricing has trained users to expect low prices, pressuring margins.

## Competitive Analysis & Moat
Competes with Coursera on breadth but with a marketplace (not curated-partner) model; closer in spirit to a content aggregator than a structured-curriculum provider.

## Due Diligence Benchmarking Use
A cautionary comparable on marketplace quality-control challenges and discount-driven pricing pressure when evaluating course-marketplace startups.
