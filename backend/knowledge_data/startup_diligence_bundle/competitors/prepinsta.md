# PrepInsta

**Category:** Competitor Intelligence Profile  
**Source Document:** `competitor_intelligence_detailed.pdf` (Pages 4, 5)

## Industry Context
India-focused campus placement preparation platform helping engineering students prepare for on-campus recruitment drives.

## Product Offering
Company-specific placement guides, aptitude and coding practice tailored to known campus-hiring test patterns, and video courses.

## Competitive Strengths
Narrow focus on campus placements (rather than general upskilling) makes it highly relevant to its specific student audience during placement season.

## Weaknesses & Vulnerabilities
Seasonal demand tied to campus recruitment cycles creates revenue volatility; narrower addressable market than broader upskilling platforms.

## Competitive Analysis & Moat
Competes with GeeksforGeeks for the same student audience but is more tightly scoped to placement-specific content rather than general CS learning.

## Due Diligence Benchmarking Use
A useful example of a seasonally-cyclical, narrowly-scoped edtech niche when assessing revenue predictability.
