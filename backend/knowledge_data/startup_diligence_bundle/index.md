## Risks (4 entities)

- **[Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) Trap](risks/customer-acquisition-cost-trap.md)** (`concept`): Reliance on paid digital acquisition channels where rising ad auction prices outpace customer lifetime value (LTV). *(Source: p.1)*
- **[Hardware Iteration Lag & Manufacturing Delays](risks/hardware-manufacturing-delay.md)** (`concept`): Prolonged BOM cost structures, tooling delays, and manufacturing quality control defects leading to cash depletion before shipping. *(Source: p.1)*
- **[Premature Geographic Scaling](risks/premature-geographic-scaling.md)** (`concept`): Expanding into multiple regional markets before proving repeatable unit economics and network liquidity in the core origin city. *(Source: p.1)*
- **[Unit Economics Failure & Negative Gross Margins](risks/unit-economics-failure.md)** (`concept`): Operating model where cost per delivery/transaction exceeds customer contribution margin, accelerating cash burn with scale. *(Source: p.1)*
