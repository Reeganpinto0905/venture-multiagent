# Atlassian

**Category:** Startup Due Diligence Case Study  
**Outcome:** `SUCCESSFUL`  
**Source Document:** `startup_successes_detailed.pdf` (Pages 5)

## Executive Summary
Enterprise software company behind developer and IT-collaboration tools including Jira and Confluence.

## Business Model & Economics
Per-seat and usage-based software licensing, historically sold with minimal traditional sales-team involvement, relying on self-serve adoption and word of mouth.

## Success Factors & Competitive Moat
Built deeply sticky tools for software development and IT teams, then expanded its product suite (Jira, Confluence, Trello, Bitbucket) to cover more of the workflow.

## Growth Drivers & Flywheels
A famously low-touch, marketing- and product-led sales model that kept customer-acquisition costs low relative to traditional enterprise-software sales.

## Investor Due Diligence Lessons
A low-touch, product-led sales model can scale enterprise software revenue efficiently if the product itself is compelling enough to sell without a large sales force.

## Key Takeaway
> Deep workflow lock-in across a suite of interconnected tools can be as durable a moat as any single flagship product.
