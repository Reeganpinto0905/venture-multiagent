# Freshworks

**Category:** Startup Due Diligence Case Study  
**Outcome:** `SUCCESSFUL`  
**Source Document:** `startup_successes_detailed.pdf` (Pages 7)

## Executive Summary
India-founded enterprise software company offering customer-support, sales, and IT-service-management tools, positioned as a lower-cost alternative to incumbents like Salesforce.

## Business Model & Economics
Tiered SaaS subscription pricing significantly undercutting larger established competitors while offering comparable core functionality.

## Success Factors & Competitive Moat
Targeted small and mid-sized businesses that felt underserved or overpriced by larger incumbents, winning them with simpler, cheaper alternatives.

## Growth Drivers & Flywheels
International expansion beyond its Indian roots, a broadening suite of connected products, and a successful U.S. public listing.

## Investor Due Diligence Lessons
Being the lower-cost, simpler alternative to an entrenched enterprise incumbent can be a viable and durable strategy, not just a race-to-the-bottom.

## Key Takeaway
> Freshworks demonstrates that a startup founded outside Silicon Valley can still successfully challenge entrenched global enterprise incumbents.
