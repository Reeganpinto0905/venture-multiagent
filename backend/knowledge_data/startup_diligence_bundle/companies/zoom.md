# Zoom

**Category:** Startup Due Diligence Case Study  
**Outcome:** `SUCCESSFUL`  
**Source Document:** `startup_successes_detailed.pdf` (Pages 7, 8)

## Executive Summary
Video-conferencing platform that saw explosive growth during the COVID-19 pandemic as remote work and remote schooling surged.

## Business Model & Economics
Freemium model with time-limited free group calls and paid tiers for longer meetings, larger participant counts, and enterprise features.

## Success Factors & Competitive Moat
Prioritized call reliability and ease of joining a meeting (no account required) over feature-richness, which mattered enormously once remote work became mandatory almost overnight.

## Growth Drivers & Flywheels
Pandemic-driven demand shock, aggressive scaling of infrastructure to handle surging usage, and expansion into adjacent products (Zoom Phone, Zoom Rooms).

## Investor Due Diligence Lessons
Sudden demand shocks can validate a product's core reliability advantage almost instantly, but sustaining that growth afterward requires expanding the product beyond the original moment.

## Key Takeaway
> Zoom's growth underscores that ease of use and reliability can matter more than feature depth when a product must work for millions of non-technical users overnight.
