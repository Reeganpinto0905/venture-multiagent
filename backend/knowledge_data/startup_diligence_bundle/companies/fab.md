# Fab

**Category:** Startup Due Diligence Case Study  
**Outcome:** `FAILED`  
**Source Document:** `startup_failures_detailedv2.pdf` (Pages 2)

## Executive Summary
Flash-sale design-focused e-commerce site that grew explosively to over a billion dollars in valuation before collapsing by 2015.

## Business Model & Economics
Revenue depended on daily flash sales of design products, requiring constant new inventory sourcing and marketing spend to keep the model fresh.

## Failure Factors & Root Causes
Pivoted its business model multiple times in a short period, overspent on international expansion (including European operations) before the core U.S. model was even proven, and could not sustain the flash-sale format's operational complexity.

## Risk Indicators Observed
Frequent, major business-model pivots in a short time window, and international expansion preceding proof of core-market unit economics.

## Due Diligence Lessons for Investors
Investors should be cautious of international expansion undertaken before a company has proven a repeatable, profitable model in its home market.

## Key Takeaway
> Expanding geographically before nailing the core business model at home usually multiplies existing problems rather than solving them.
