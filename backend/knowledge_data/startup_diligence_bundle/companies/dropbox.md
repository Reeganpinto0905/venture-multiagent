# Dropbox

**Category:** Startup Due Diligence Case Study  
**Outcome:** `SUCCESSFUL`  
**Source Document:** `startup_successes_detailed.pdf` (Pages 2)

## Executive Summary
Cloud file storage and synchronization service that lets files stay updated across multiple devices automatically.

## Business Model & Economics
Freemium storage tiers with paid upgrades for more space and business-focused collaboration features.

## Success Factors & Competitive Moat
Extremely simple, 'it just works' file-sync experience won users when technical alternatives were clunky, and a referral program tied additional storage to invites.

## Growth Drivers & Flywheels
A widely imitated referral program that turned users into an acquisition channel, plus early integration into everyday workflows before larger competitors caught up.

## Investor Due Diligence Lessons
Being early and simple in a category can outweigh a competitor's bigger platform advantage, at least until that competitor prioritizes the space.

## Key Takeaway
> Simplicity of the core use case (files staying in sync) mattered more than a long feature list in the product's early growth phase.
