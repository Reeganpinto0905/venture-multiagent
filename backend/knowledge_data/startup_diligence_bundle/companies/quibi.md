# Quibi

**Category:** Startup Due Diligence Case Study  
**Outcome:** `FAILED`  
**Source Document:** `startup_failures_detailedv2.pdf` (Pages 1)

## Executive Summary
Short-form (under 10 minute), mobile-only premium streaming service launched in 2020 with major Hollywood backing.

## Business Model & Economics
Subscription revenue depended on convincing users to adopt an entirely new mobile-first viewing habit, funded by an unusually large pre-launch budget (over one billion dollars).

## Failure Factors & Root Causes
Launched without a social-sharing or screenshot feature, misjudged how people actually watch video (often on TV, not just mobile), and launched right as the pandemic emptied the commutes it was designed around.

## Risk Indicators Observed
Extremely high pre-launch spending with no revealed user demand signal, and a product design that ignored well-established consumer content-sharing habits.

## Due Diligence Lessons for Investors
Investors should test whether a product depends on a completely new consumer behavior, and treat that dependency as a major execution risk rather than a minor detail.

## Key Takeaway
> A huge budget cannot substitute for validating the core behavioral assumption a product depends on before scaling spend.
