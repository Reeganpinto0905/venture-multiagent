# MoviePass

**Category:** Startup Due Diligence Case Study  
**Outcome:** `FAILED`  
**Source Document:** `startup_failures_detailedv2.pdf` (Pages 7, 8)

## Executive Summary
Subscription service offering unlimited (or near-unlimited) movie-theater tickets for a flat low monthly fee, which saw explosive signups in 2017-2018.

## Business Model & Economics
Flat monthly subscription fee that was, for a period, priced below the cost of a single movie ticket, while MoviePass still paid theaters full price per ticket used.

## Failure Factors & Root Causes
The pricing model paid out more per subscriber than it collected in revenue whenever a subscriber used the service more than once a month, an unsustainable structure that consumed cash rapidly as adoption grew.

## Risk Indicators Observed
A pricing structure where increased customer usage directly increases the company's losses rather than its revenue.

## Due Diligence Lessons for Investors
Investors should be highly suspicious of a pricing model where using the product more frequently makes the company lose more money, since growth in that case accelerates losses rather than revenue.

## Key Takeaway
> Rapid subscriber growth is a red flag, not a green flag, when a company's unit economics mean each additional use of the product loses money.
