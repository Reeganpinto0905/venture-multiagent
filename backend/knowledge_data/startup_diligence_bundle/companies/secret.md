# Secret

**Category:** Startup Due Diligence Case Study  
**Outcome:** `FAILED`  
**Source Document:** `startup_failures_detailedv2.pdf` (Pages 7)

## Executive Summary
Anonymous social-sharing app that let users post messages visible to their contacts without revealing their identity, before shutting down in 2015.

## Business Model & Economics
Free consumer social app with no monetization built in, betting that scale and engagement would eventually justify a business model.

## Failure Factors & Root Causes
Anonymity led to widespread bullying, harassment, and misinformation on the platform, creating reputational and moderation problems the founders could not solve, while a co-founder dispute added further instability.

## Risk Indicators Observed
A platform design (anonymity) that structurally invites abuse, without a credible moderation plan in place before scaling.

## Due Diligence Lessons for Investors
Investors should specifically diligence trust-and-safety and content-moderation plans for any platform built around anonymity, since these risks can end a company faster than a competitive threat.

## Key Takeaway
> For social platforms, trust-and-safety risk deserves the same diligence rigor as growth metrics, since it can single-handedly end the business.
