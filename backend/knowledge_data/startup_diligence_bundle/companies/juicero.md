# Juicero

**Category:** Startup Due Diligence Case Study  
**Outcome:** `FAILED`  
**Source Document:** `startup_failures_detailedv2.pdf` (Pages 3, 4)

## Executive Summary
Maker of an internet-connected cold-press juicer that required proprietary pre-packaged juice packs, backed by over 100 million dollars in funding.

## Business Model & Economics
Sold an expensive hardware press (originally around 700 dollars) subsidized by recurring sales of proprietary juice packs, similar to a razor-and-blade model.

## Failure Factors & Root Causes
Press became infamous after reports showed the juice packs could be squeezed by hand just as effectively without the machine, eliminating the product's core value proposition overnight.

## Risk Indicators Observed
A premium hardware product whose core function can be replicated by a simple manual alternative at a fraction of the cost.

## Due Diligence Lessons for Investors
Investors should personally validate that a product's core mechanism provides value a much cheaper or manual alternative cannot replicate.

## Key Takeaway
> Sophisticated engineering and funding cannot compensate for a core value proposition that does not actually require the product to exist.
