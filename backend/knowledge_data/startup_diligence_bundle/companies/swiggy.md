# Swiggy

**Category:** Startup Due Diligence Case Study  
**Outcome:** `SUCCESSFUL`  
**Source Document:** `startup_successes_detailed.pdf` (Pages 3, 4)

## Executive Summary
India-based food delivery platform connecting restaurants, delivery partners, and customers.

## Business Model & Economics
Commission on orders from restaurant partners plus delivery fees from customers, later expanded into instant grocery delivery (Instamart).

## Success Factors & Competitive Moat
Built dense delivery-partner networks city by city to guarantee fast delivery times, which became a key differentiator against slower competitors.

## Growth Drivers & Flywheels
Expansion into adjacent categories (groceries, dining-out platform) and continued densification of delivery infrastructure in existing cities.

## Investor Due Diligence Lessons
Logistics density (delivery speed and reliability) can be as important a moat as the marketplace itself in food-delivery businesses.

## Key Takeaway
> Category expansion using existing delivery infrastructure is a common and capital-efficient path to growth once core logistics are proven.
