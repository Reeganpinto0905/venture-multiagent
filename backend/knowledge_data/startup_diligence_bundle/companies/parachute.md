# Parachute

**Category:** Startup Due Diligence Case Study  
**Outcome:** `FAILED`  
**Source Document:** `startup_failures_detailedv2.pdf` (Pages 8)

## Executive Summary
Direct-to-consumer home goods and bedding brand that filed for bankruptcy in 2023 after early rapid growth in the DTC boom.

## Business Model & Economics
Sold premium bedding and home goods directly to consumers online, relying heavily on paid digital advertising for customer acquisition.

## Failure Factors & Root Causes
Rising digital-advertising costs steadily eroded customer-acquisition economics across the DTC sector, and Parachute's growth had been built on acquisition costs that were no longer sustainable once ad prices rose.

## Risk Indicators Observed
A direct-to-consumer model heavily reliant on paid digital advertising, in a category where ad costs have been rising faster than pricing power.

## Due Diligence Lessons for Investors
Investors should track customer-acquisition-cost trends for a sector as a whole, not just a single company's current numbers, since industry-wide ad-cost inflation can erode many DTC brands' economics simultaneously.

## Key Takeaway
> DTC brands built primarily on paid-advertising-driven growth are exposed to industry-wide, not just company-specific, acquisition-cost risk.
