# Slack

**Category:** Startup Due Diligence Case Study  
**Outcome:** `SUCCESSFUL`  
**Source Document:** `startup_successes_detailed.pdf` (Pages 5)

## Executive Summary
Business messaging and collaboration platform organizing team communication into channels.

## Business Model & Economics
Freemium/subscription model priced per active user, with paid tiers unlocking message history, integrations, and admin controls.

## Success Factors & Competitive Moat
Made internal company communication far more organized and searchable than email, and its integration ecosystem made it a hub for other workplace tools.

## Growth Drivers & Flywheels
Bottom-up adoption within individual teams before expanding to full-company rollouts, plus a rich app/integration marketplace that increased switching costs.

## Investor Due Diligence Lessons
Bottom-up, team-level adoption can be a powerful go-to-market motion for B2B software, letting a product prove value before an enterprise-wide sales push.

## Key Takeaway
> Becoming the communication 'hub' that other workplace tools integrate into can create durable switching costs beyond the core product.
