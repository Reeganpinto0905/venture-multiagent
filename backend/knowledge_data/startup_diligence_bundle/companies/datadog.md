# Datadog

**Category:** Startup Due Diligence Case Study  
**Outcome:** `SUCCESSFUL`  
**Source Document:** `startup_successes_detailed.pdf` (Pages 8)

## Executive Summary
Cloud monitoring and observability platform that tracks infrastructure, application performance, and logs for engineering teams.

## Business Model & Economics
Usage-based SaaS pricing tied to the volume of infrastructure and data being monitored, which scales naturally as customers' cloud footprints grow.

## Success Factors & Competitive Moat
Built a genuinely unified platform across infrastructure monitoring, APM, and logging when many competitors offered only point solutions, reducing tool sprawl for customers.

## Growth Drivers & Flywheels
Land-and-expand sales motion (start with one monitoring product, expand into the full suite), and usage-based pricing that grows automatically alongside customer cloud usage.

## Investor Due Diligence Lessons
Usage-based pricing tied to a customer's own growth (cloud infrastructure scale) can create a revenue model that expands without additional sales effort.

## Key Takeaway
> Consolidating multiple point-solution categories into one platform can be a strong wedge against fragmented incumbents.
