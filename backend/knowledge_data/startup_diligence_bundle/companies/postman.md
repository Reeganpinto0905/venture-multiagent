# Postman

**Category:** Startup Due Diligence Case Study  
**Outcome:** `SUCCESSFUL`  
**Source Document:** `startup_successes_detailed.pdf` (Pages 7)

## Executive Summary
API development and testing platform used by developers to build, test, and document APIs.

## Business Model & Economics
Freemium model for individual developers with paid team and enterprise tiers adding collaboration, governance, and API-management features.

## Success Factors & Competitive Moat
Became the de facto standard tool in developers' daily workflow by being genuinely useful for free first, then expanding into team collaboration.

## Growth Drivers & Flywheels
Strong bottom-up, developer-led adoption before any enterprise sales motion, plus expansion into a broader API platform (mock servers, monitoring, documentation).

## Investor Due Diligence Lessons
Bottom-up developer adoption of a genuinely useful free tool can be a more durable growth engine than top-down enterprise sales in developer-tooling markets.

## Key Takeaway
> Owning a daily developer workflow habit early can be leveraged into a much larger platform business over time.
