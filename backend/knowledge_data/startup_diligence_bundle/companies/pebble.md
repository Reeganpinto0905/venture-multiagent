# Pebble

**Category:** Startup Due Diligence Case Study  
**Outcome:** `FAILED`  
**Source Document:** `startup_failures_detailedv2.pdf` (Pages 3)

## Executive Summary
Early pioneer in the smartwatch category, known for a successful Kickstarter launch and an e-paper display, before its assets were acquired by Fitbit in 2016.

## Business Model & Economics
Hardware sales of smartwatches, competing on price and openness against Apple and later well-funded Android-ecosystem competitors.

## Failure Factors & Root Causes
Could not match the resources and ecosystem lock-in of Apple Watch once Apple entered the category, and hardware margins were too thin to fund the R&D; needed to keep pace.

## Risk Indicators Observed
A hardware startup competing directly against a much larger platform company entering the same space with deeper pockets and an existing ecosystem.

## Due Diligence Lessons for Investors
Investors should assess whether a hardware startup has a durable technical or ecosystem advantage that survives a much larger competitor entering the same category.

## Key Takeaway
> Being first to a hardware category provides little protection once a dominant platform company decides to compete directly in it.
