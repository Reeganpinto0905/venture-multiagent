# Homejoy

**Category:** Startup Due Diligence Case Study  
**Outcome:** `FAILED`  
**Source Document:** `startup_failures_detailedv2.pdf` (Pages 2)

## Executive Summary
On-demand home-cleaning marketplace connecting customers with independent cleaners, once considered a leading 'Uber for X' startup.

## Business Model & Economics
Took a commission on each cleaning booking, subsidizing customer acquisition heavily through discount promotions to drive early growth.

## Failure Factors & Root Causes
Extremely low customer retention after promotional discounts expired, combined with lawsuits over worker classification of its cleaners as independent contractors.

## Risk Indicators Observed
Retention that collapses once promotional pricing ends, and unresolved labor-classification legal exposure in a gig-worker-dependent model.

## Due Diligence Lessons for Investors
Investors should separate growth driven by heavy discounting from organic, retained demand, since the two look identical in early top-line numbers.

## Key Takeaway
> Discount-fueled growth can disguise a lack of genuine repeat demand until the promotions stop and the real retention curve is revealed.
