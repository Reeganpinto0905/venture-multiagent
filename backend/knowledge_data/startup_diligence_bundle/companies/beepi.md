# Beepi

**Category:** Startup Due Diligence Case Study  
**Outcome:** `FAILED`  
**Source Document:** `startup_failures_detailedv2.pdf` (Pages 1, 2)

## Executive Summary
Peer-to-peer used-car marketplace that promised end-to-end online buying and selling with home delivery, before shutting down in 2017.

## Business Model & Economics
Revenue depended on transaction fees from car sales, but the company also bought and held inventory itself, which is extremely capital-intensive.

## Failure Factors & Root Causes
Burned through roughly 150 million dollars in funding on marketing and inventory while the actual unit economics of buying, refurbishing, and delivering cars never worked, and cofounder conflict compounded the strain.

## Risk Indicators Observed
A marketplace pitch that in practice requires holding physical inventory, and spending significantly outpacing any path to per-unit profitability.

## Due Diligence Lessons for Investors
Investors should scrutinize whether a marketplace is truly asset-light or secretly carrying expensive physical inventory, since that changes the entire risk profile.

## Key Takeaway
> A 'marketplace' label can mask an inventory-heavy, capital-intensive business model that behaves nothing like a true asset-light marketplace.
