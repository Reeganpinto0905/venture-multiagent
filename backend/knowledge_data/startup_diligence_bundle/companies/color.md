# Color

**Category:** Startup Due Diligence Case Study  
**Outcome:** `FAILED`  
**Source Document:** `startup_failures_detailedv2.pdf` (Pages 4)

## Executive Summary
Photo-sharing mobile app that raised an unusually large 41 million dollars in seed funding before launch in 2011, based mainly on the founding team's pedigree.

## Business Model & Economics
Free consumer social app intended to monetize later through scale and engagement, but never articulated a clear path to either.

## Failure Factors & Root Causes
Confusing user experience that most people did not understand or want, launched with an outsized funding round that created pressure for immediate massive-scale adoption that never materialized.

## Risk Indicators Observed
A consumer app whose core use case is not clearly understood by early users, paired with funding far in excess of what the validated traction supports.

## Due Diligence Lessons for Investors
Investors should be wary of unusually large early-stage rounds justified primarily by founder pedigree rather than validated product-market fit.

## Key Takeaway
> Founder pedigree and funding size are not substitutes for a product concept that ordinary users actually understand and want to use.
