# Stripe

**Category:** Startup Due Diligence Case Study  
**Outcome:** `SUCCESSFUL`  
**Source Document:** `startup_successes_detailed.pdf` (Pages 1)

## Executive Summary
Payments infrastructure company providing APIs that let businesses accept and manage online payments.

## Business Model & Economics
Usage-based transaction fees charged to businesses processing payments through Stripe's APIs, expanding into billing, fraud prevention, and treasury products.

## Success Factors & Competitive Moat
Developer-first design made integration dramatically faster than incumbent payment processors, turning technical simplicity into a genuine competitive moat.

## Growth Drivers & Flywheels
Expansion from a single payments API into a broad financial-infrastructure suite, plus deep partnerships with platforms that embedded Stripe by default.

## Investor Due Diligence Lessons
A superior developer experience can be a durable moat in infrastructure markets, since switching costs rise once a product is deeply integrated into engineering workflows.

## Key Takeaway
> Investing in the unglamorous parts of a market (integration friction, documentation quality) can be as valuable as the core feature set.
