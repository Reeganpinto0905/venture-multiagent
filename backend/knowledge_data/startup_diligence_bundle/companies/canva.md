# Canva

**Category:** Startup Due Diligence Case Study  
**Outcome:** `SUCCESSFUL`  
**Source Document:** `startup_successes_detailed.pdf` (Pages 1, 2)

## Executive Summary
Online graphic design platform aimed at non-designers who need to create visual content quickly.

## Business Model & Economics
Freemium model with a free tier for basic design and paid subscriptions unlocking premium templates, stock assets, and team features.

## Success Factors & Competitive Moat
Drag-and-drop simplicity opened design tools to a massive non-designer audience that tools like Photoshop had never served.

## Growth Drivers & Flywheels
Viral, template-driven sharing loops, expansion into video and presentations, and a strong push into enterprise/team plans.

## Investor Due Diligence Lessons
A large underserved non-expert segment can be more valuable than competing head-on for the smaller professional-expert segment an incumbent already owns.

## Key Takeaway
> Removing complexity, not adding features, was Canva's core innovation relative to professional design tools.
