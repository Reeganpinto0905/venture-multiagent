# Sprig

**Category:** Startup Due Diligence Case Study  
**Outcome:** `FAILED`  
**Source Document:** `startup_failures_detailedv2.pdf` (Pages 6)

## Executive Summary
On-demand, chef-prepared meal delivery startup that raised over 55 million dollars before shutting down in 2017.

## Business Model & Economics
Sold prepared meals directly to consumers through its own kitchens and delivery fleet, taking on significant fixed costs of food production and logistics simultaneously.

## Failure Factors & Root Causes
Operating its own kitchens and delivery fleet created a high fixed-cost structure that could not be justified by order volume, and unit economics never turned positive even after several years of operation.

## Risk Indicators Observed
A company bearing both production costs (owned kitchens) and logistics costs (owned delivery) simultaneously, with volumes too low to cover either.

## Due Diligence Lessons for Investors
Investors should be cautious of 'full-stack' models that take on both production and logistics costs at once, since it doubles the operational execution risk relative to a pure marketplace.

## Key Takeaway
> Vertically-integrated 'full-stack' consumer startups face a much higher operational execution bar than asset-light marketplace competitors in the same category.
