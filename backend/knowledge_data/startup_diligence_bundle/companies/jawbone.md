# Jawbone

**Category:** Startup Due Diligence Case Study  
**Outcome:** `FAILED`  
**Source Document:** `startup_failures_detailedv2.pdf` (Pages 1)

## Executive Summary
Wearable fitness-tracker and Bluetooth-speaker hardware company that raised close to a billion dollars before shutting down in 2017.

## Business Model & Economics
Hardware sales dependent on constant new product releases in an increasingly crowded wearables market against Fitbit and, later, Apple Watch.

## Failure Factors & Root Causes
Chronic product-quality and reliability issues, missed shipping deadlines, and a hardware cost structure that could not compete once Fitbit and Apple entered aggressively.

## Risk Indicators Observed
Repeated product recalls or delays, and a shrinking gap between a startup's hardware and a much larger competitor's equivalent offering.

## Due Diligence Lessons for Investors
In hardware, investors should specifically diligence manufacturing reliability and shipping track record, not just design and marketing appeal.

## Key Takeaway
> Hardware startups face a much less forgiving cost and reliability bar than software, and being early is not enough if quality control lags.
