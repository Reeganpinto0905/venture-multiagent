# Airbnb

**Category:** Startup Due Diligence Case Study  
**Outcome:** `SUCCESSFUL`  
**Source Document:** `startup_successes_detailed.pdf` (Pages 1)

## Executive Summary
Online marketplace connecting travelers with hosts renting out homes, rooms, and unique stays worldwide.

## Business Model & Economics
Commission-based marketplace taking a cut from both hosts and guests on each booking, with no inventory of its own.

## Success Factors & Competitive Moat
Solved a genuine trust problem in short-term rentals through reviews, verified profiles, and host guarantees, letting it scale supply faster than hotels could match.

## Growth Drivers & Flywheels
Powerful network effects between hosts and guests, expansion into 'Experiences' and long-term stays, and a strong brand that became synonymous with the category.

## Investor Due Diligence Lessons
Marketplace businesses can achieve outsized valuations once supply and demand liquidity reach critical mass in enough cities simultaneously.

## Key Takeaway
> Trust and safety infrastructure, not just inventory growth, was the real unlock that let a two-sided marketplace scale globally.
