# Zerodha

**Category:** Startup Due Diligence Case Study  
**Outcome:** `SUCCESSFUL`  
**Source Document:** `startup_successes_detailed.pdf` (Pages 2, 3)

## Executive Summary
India-based discount stock brokerage offering low-cost trading and investing through its own trading platforms.

## Business Model & Economics
Flat-fee brokerage model (rather than percentage-based commissions) that made frequent trading dramatically cheaper for retail investors.

## Success Factors & Competitive Moat
Bootstrapped without external venture funding, relying on strong unit economics from its flat-fee model rather than subsidized growth.

## Growth Drivers & Flywheels
Word-of-mouth reputation among retail traders, investment in its own technology stack (Kite platform), and an education arm (Varsity) that built trust with new investors.

## Investor Due Diligence Lessons
A profitable, low-cost pricing model can outcompete better-funded rivals if it's paired with a genuinely better product experience.

## Key Takeaway
> Zerodha shows that a startup doesn't need heavy venture funding to win a market if its core pricing and product advantage is structural, not subsidized.
