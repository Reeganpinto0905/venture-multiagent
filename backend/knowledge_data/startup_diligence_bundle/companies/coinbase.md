# Coinbase

**Category:** Startup Due Diligence Case Study  
**Outcome:** `SUCCESSFUL`  
**Source Document:** `startup_successes_detailed.pdf` (Pages 6)

## Executive Summary
Cryptocurrency exchange and custodial platform for buying, selling, and storing digital assets.

## Business Model & Economics
Trading fees on cryptocurrency transactions, plus subscription and custody products for institutional and retail customers.

## Success Factors & Competitive Moat
Prioritized regulatory compliance and ease of use earlier than many competitors, which built trust with mainstream (non-crypto-native) users entering the space.

## Growth Drivers & Flywheels
Being one of the first crypto exchanges to go public, expansion into institutional custody, and riding multiple crypto market growth cycles.

## Investor Due Diligence Lessons
In a nascent, loosely regulated category, being early to prioritize compliance and trust can be a durable advantage once the category matures and scrutiny increases.

## Key Takeaway
> Coinbase's growth shows how regulatory-first positioning can become a competitive moat in an industry where trust is scarce.
