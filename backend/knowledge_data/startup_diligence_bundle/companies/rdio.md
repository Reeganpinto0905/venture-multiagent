# Rdio

**Category:** Startup Due Diligence Case Study  
**Outcome:** `FAILED`  
**Source Document:** `startup_failures_detailedv2.pdf` (Pages 6)

## Executive Summary
Music-streaming subscription service that competed directly with Spotify before filing for bankruptcy in 2015 and having its assets acquired by Pandora.

## Business Model & Economics
Subscription-based streaming model nearly identical to Spotify's, competing in a market where licensing costs are high and margins are thin regardless of scale.

## Failure Factors & Root Causes
Could never reach the scale advantage Spotify achieved, while paying essentially the same steep music-licensing costs, leaving it permanently underwater on unit economics relative to its larger competitor.

## Risk Indicators Observed
Competing against a much larger, better-funded direct competitor in a category where licensing or fixed costs do not meaningfully decrease at smaller scale.

## Due Diligence Lessons for Investors
Investors should assess whether a startup in a scale-dependent, high-fixed-cost category (like music licensing) can realistically reach the scale its larger competitors already have.

## Key Takeaway
> In categories with steep fixed licensing costs, being a smaller, slower-growing player in a winner-take-most market is a structurally difficult position.
