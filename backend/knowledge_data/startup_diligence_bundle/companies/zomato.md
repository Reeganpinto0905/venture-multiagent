# Zomato

**Category:** Startup Due Diligence Case Study  
**Outcome:** `SUCCESSFUL`  
**Source Document:** `startup_successes_detailed.pdf` (Pages 4)

## Executive Summary
India-based food delivery and restaurant discovery platform, also engaged in quick commerce through its Blinkit acquisition.

## Business Model & Economics
Commission on food delivery orders and advertising revenue from restaurants, later diversified into quick commerce.

## Success Factors & Competitive Moat
Started as a restaurant-discovery and review platform before pivoting into delivery, giving it an early trust and content advantage over pure logistics-first competitors.

## Growth Drivers & Flywheels
Strategic acquisitions (including Blinkit) to diversify into quick commerce, plus continued investment in delivery infrastructure and restaurant partnerships.

## Investor Due Diligence Lessons
A content/discovery advantage built early in a category can be leveraged later into a full transactional marketplace.

## Key Takeaway
> Diversifying into an adjacent high-growth category (quick commerce) can extend a company's growth curve once its core market matures.
