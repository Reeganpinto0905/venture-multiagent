# Figma

**Category:** Startup Due Diligence Case Study  
**Outcome:** `SUCCESSFUL`  
**Source Document:** `startup_successes_detailed.pdf` (Pages 4, 5)

## Executive Summary
Browser-based collaborative interface design tool that lets multiple designers work on the same file simultaneously.

## Business Model & Economics
Freemium model with paid tiers for teams needing more collaboration seats and enterprise features; acquired by Adobe in a landmark 2022 deal (later abandoned amid regulatory pushback, with Figma remaining independent).

## Success Factors & Competitive Moat
Real-time, multiplayer collaboration in the browser solved a persistent pain point in design workflows that desktop-only tools like Sketch could not match.

## Growth Drivers & Flywheels
Word-of-mouth adoption among design teams, deep integration into product-development workflows, and the resulting strategic interest from Adobe.

## Investor Due Diligence Lessons
Collaboration as a core architecture (not a bolted-on feature) can be a defensible differentiator against entrenched desktop-first incumbents.

## Key Takeaway
> Figma illustrates how a fundamentally different technical architecture (real-time multiplayer in-browser) can unseat an entrenched incumbent's category.
