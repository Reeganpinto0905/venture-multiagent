# Shopify

**Category:** Startup Due Diligence Case Study  
**Outcome:** `SUCCESSFUL`  
**Source Document:** `startup_successes_detailed.pdf` (Pages 6)

## Executive Summary
E-commerce platform letting merchants of any size set up and run online stores.

## Business Model & Economics
Subscription tiers plus transaction fees and an app marketplace/payments ecosystem generating additional revenue per merchant.

## Success Factors & Competitive Moat
Lowered the technical barrier to launching an online store dramatically, letting small merchants compete with much larger e-commerce operations.

## Growth Drivers & Flywheels
A large third-party app and theme ecosystem, expansion into point-of-sale and fulfillment, and partnerships that embedded Shopify checkout across the web.

## Investor Due Diligence Lessons
Platform businesses that let third parties build on top of them (apps, themes) can grow faster than a company building every feature itself.

## Key Takeaway
> Removing technical barriers for an underserved segment (small merchants) can create a very large addressable market over time.
