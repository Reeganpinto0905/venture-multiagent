# TinyOwl

**Category:** Startup Due Diligence Case Study  
**Outcome:** `FAILED`  
**Source Document:** `startup_failures_detailedv2.pdf` (Pages 5, 6)

## Executive Summary
India-based food-ordering and delivery startup that scaled rapidly across multiple cities before collapsing in 2016.

## Business Model & Economics
Commission-based food-delivery marketplace model, expanding aggressively into many Indian cities simultaneously to compete with Swiggy and Zomato.

## Failure Factors & Root Causes
Expanded into too many cities before operations in any single city were profitable, leading to a much-publicized wave of layoffs and office protests as funding dried up.

## Risk Indicators Observed
Simultaneous expansion into numerous markets with none individually profitable, and funding that dries up before any single market matures.

## Due Diligence Lessons for Investors
Investors should verify that unit economics are proven in at least one market before funding aggressive multi-city expansion.

## Key Takeaway
> Expanding geographic footprint before proving unit economics in even one market multiplies losses rather than de-risking the business.
