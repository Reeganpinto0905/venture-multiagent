# Razorpay

**Category:** Startup Due Diligence Case Study  
**Outcome:** `SUCCESSFUL`  
**Source Document:** `startup_successes_detailed.pdf` (Pages 3)

## Executive Summary
India-based payments and business banking platform for online and offline businesses.

## Business Model & Economics
Transaction fees on payments processed, expanded into business banking, payroll, and lending products for the same customer base.

## Success Factors & Competitive Moat
Rode India's shift to digital payments by offering an easy-to-integrate gateway for businesses of all sizes, then expanded 'horizontally' into adjacent financial products for the same customers.

## Growth Drivers & Flywheels
Rapid expansion of its product suite (from payments into neo-banking and lending) increased revenue per customer without needing new customer acquisition.

## Investor Due Diligence Lessons
Expanding the product suite within an existing customer base can grow revenue faster and more efficiently than only acquiring new customers.

## Key Takeaway
> Owning the core transaction relationship with a customer creates room to sell additional financial products over time.
