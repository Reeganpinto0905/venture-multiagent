# Notion

**Category:** Startup Due Diligence Case Study  
**Outcome:** `SUCCESSFUL`  
**Source Document:** `startup_successes_detailed.pdf` (Pages 4)

## Executive Summary
All-in-one workspace app combining notes, docs, wikis, project management, and databases in a single flexible tool.

## Business Model & Economics
Freemium model with individual free use and paid team/enterprise subscriptions for collaboration features and larger workspaces.

## Success Factors & Competitive Moat
Highly flexible, block-based editor let users build their own workflows instead of forcing a rigid structure, driving strong organic and community-led growth.

## Growth Drivers & Flywheels
A passionate user community that created and shared templates, viral use-case content on social media, and steady expansion into team and enterprise use cases.

## Investor Due Diligence Lessons
Flexible, 'build your own workflow' tools can generate strong organic virality through user-created content (templates, tutorials) without heavy paid marketing.

## Key Takeaway
> Letting users shape the product to their own use cases can be a stronger growth engine than shipping a fixed feature set.
