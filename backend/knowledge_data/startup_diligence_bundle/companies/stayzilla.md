# Stayzilla

**Category:** Startup Due Diligence Case Study  
**Outcome:** `FAILED`  
**Source Document:** `startup_failures_detailedv2.pdf` (Pages 5)

## Executive Summary
India-based budget homestay and accommodation booking platform that shut down in 2017 after failing to secure further funding.

## Business Model & Economics
Commission-based marketplace model connecting travelers with budget homestays, competing against larger hotel-booking platforms and, later, Airbnb's India entry.

## Failure Factors & Root Causes
Could not raise a further funding round as investor sentiment cooled toward India's travel-marketplace segment, and disputes with vendors over unpaid dues became public and damaged its reputation during the shutdown.

## Risk Indicators Observed
A funding shutdown that becomes entangled with vendor payment disputes, amplifying reputational damage beyond the underlying business failure.

## Due Diligence Lessons for Investors
Investors should examine how a startup treats vendor and partner payment obligations during a downturn, since public disputes can compound reputational and legal risk on top of the original funding shortfall.

## Key Takeaway
> How a company manages the shutdown process itself, not just why it ran out of money, can materially affect founders' and investors' outcomes and reputations.
