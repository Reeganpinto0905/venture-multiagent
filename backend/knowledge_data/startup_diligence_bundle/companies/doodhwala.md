# Doodhwala

**Category:** Startup Due Diligence Case Study  
**Outcome:** `FAILED`  
**Source Document:** `startup_failures_detailedv2.pdf` (Pages 4, 5)

## Executive Summary
India-based daily milk and grocery delivery subscription startup that shut down in 2019 after failing to raise a planned funding round.

## Business Model & Economics
Subscription-based recurring delivery of milk and groceries, competing on convenience against traditional local delivery and larger grocery-delivery apps.

## Failure Factors & Root Causes
Thin margins inherent to daily essentials delivery meant the model depended entirely on reaching massive scale, and a planned funding round fell through, leaving the company unable to sustain operations.

## Risk Indicators Observed
A business model with structurally thin per-unit margins that is entirely dependent on continuous new funding rather than near-term profitability.

## Due Diligence Lessons for Investors
Investors should stress-test what happens to a low-margin, scale-dependent business if a planned follow-on funding round does not close on schedule.

## Key Takeaway
> Low-margin, high-volume consumer businesses are especially vulnerable to funding-market timing, since they typically cannot self-fund through a gap in fundraising.
